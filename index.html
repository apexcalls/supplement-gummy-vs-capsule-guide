Gummies Versus Capsules: Which Supplement Form Suits You?

Gummies are popular because they taste pleasant and feel easy to take, but that convenience comes with trade-offs. Gummies need sweeteners, gelling agents, and flavourings to hold their shape, which leaves less room for active ingredients. A single gummy serving may therefore contain a smaller dose than an equivalent capsule, and some gummies add sugar that buyers trying to limit sugar intake would rather avoid.

Capsules, by contrast, can hold larger doses with fewer extras, but they are harder for some people to swallow, and their coatings can affect how quickly contents are released. Neither form is universally better. The right choice depends on the dose you need, whether you can swallow capsules comfortably, and whether added sugars or fillers matter to you.
